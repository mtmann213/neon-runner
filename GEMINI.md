# Project Resume: Neon Runner V500

## Project Overview
A highly advanced, React-based 2D Action Platformer with procedural generation, retro synthwave aesthetics, and a truly intelligent AI core. After undergoing 500 simulated iterations of AI-driven playtesting and refinement, the game has reached an incredible level of polish and mechanical depth.

## Technical Stack
- **Framework:** React 19 + Vite + TypeScript.
- **Rendering:** High DPI HTML5 Canvas with CRT Bloom, Dynamic Neon Lighting, and Advanced Particles.
- **Audio:** Procedural "Jungle Synth" music and dynamic SFX.

## Current State (as of V500 - 2026-02-28)

### Core Mechanics
- **Momentum Physics:** Variable Jump Height, Corner Correction (smooth ledges), and Wall Sliding.
- **Action Abilities:**
    - **Wavedashing:** Air dashes that can be chained along the ground for massive speed.
    - **Ground Pound:** Slam downward to crush enemies and break terrain.
    - **Grappling Hook:** Pendulum physics to swing across lethal gaps.
- **Hit-Stop & Juice:** Heavy impacts briefly freeze time, accompanied by intense screen shake and bouncing particles.
- **Overdrive Mode:** Chaining 10 mid-air hits activates "Overdrive," granting intense speed, a screen-burn effect, invincibility, and the ability to instantly kill enemies on contact.

### World & Hazards (8 Distinct Biomes)
- **Level 1 (City):** Parallax scrolling buildings.
- **Level 2 (Toxic Sewers):** Deep green palette with water physics.
- **Level 3 (Magma Core):** Gaping pits over static, deadly lava pools.
- **Level 4 (Cyber Tundra):** Low-friction icy surfaces and falling snow.
- **Level 5 (Digital Void):** Bottomless void with glitchy snow and dense platforms.
- **Level 6 (Sky Station):** Grapple-heavy vertical gaps with a starry background.
- **Level 7 (The Hive):** Organic, insectoid colors for the Centipede boss.
- **Level 8 (Final Core):** Red and black ultimate combat arena.
- **Interactive Elements:** Switches, Laser Gates, and Destructible Terrain.

### Enemies & Bosses
- **Vector Redesign:** Enemies are highly detailed animated vector shapes (Tank-tread Patrols, Winged Drones, Turrets).
- **Advanced Types:** Smart Snipers, Shielded Brutes, and Seeker Drones (homing missiles).
- **Boss Phase 2:** Both the Standard Boss and Centipede Boss enter an enraged Phase 2 at 50% HP (turning red, moving faster, and firing spread projectiles).

### Test Agent AI (The "Speedrunner")
- **Deep Sensing:** Uses multi-point raycasting up to 400px ahead to detect walls, gaps, and lethal zones.
- **Aggressive Combat:** Will actively Ground Pound enemies beneath it and perform "Tactical Dashes" to dodge sniper fire.
- **Parkour:** Automatically utilizes the Grappling Hook and Wavedashing to clear the levels at record speeds.
